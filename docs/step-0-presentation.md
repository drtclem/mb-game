# Step 0 presentation baseline

The reconstruction uses midnight navy (#0a1724), muted turf green, ice-blue band uniforms and warm gold for the player. System sans-serif displays and monospace labels require no font download. Generated geometry supplies uniforms, brass horns, turf, hashes, field lines and simple stands; hemisphere and stadium directional lighting keep the field readable. HUD, menu and recap share the same palette and border treatment.

This baseline supports review of the gameplay slice. The locked stylized-realistic hero details, instanced band, stadium detail and full art style sheet remain the separate step 2 pass.
