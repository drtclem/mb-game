# Game Week brief (summary)

Summary of `Game_Week_Assignment.pdf`. The PDF is the source of truth.

## Schedule
- Game due **Thursday at midnight**.
- Playing and judging **Friday morning**; winners announced around lunch.

## Central focus
A beautiful, unique, clean game. Practice generating the visual and audio assets that give the game its identity, not just making mechanics work.

## Bar for every game
- **Polished, cohesive graphics (pass/fail).** Thrown-together or mismatched art fails.
- **Substantial:** a complete experience with varied challenges and progression, not a demo or simple clone.
- **Clear objective** and how to play.
- **Beginning, middle and end.**
- **More than one moment of gameplay:** new challenges, choices or mechanics as you progress.
- **Unique, clean design:** look, sound and feel reinforce the world.
- **Playable by someone else** without help; brief instructions where needed.
- **Browser access:** a link, no install.
- **Finish the details:** layout, typography, animation, sound levels, feedback.
- **Generate all assets:** visuals and audio (sprites, characters, environments, sound effects, music). No pre-made assets.
- **Polish over size.**

## Our option: 3, Multiplayer Racing (inspiration: Mario Kart)
Required:
- At least two people racing together, with a clear way to join the same game.
- At least two distinct tracks or courses.
- Complete flow: start, race, finish, results, play again.
- Clear feedback on position or progress and who won.
- At least one mechanic beyond basic steering and acceleration (boosts, hazards, drifting, power-ups).
- Multiplayer reliable enough to complete a race together.

What makes it great: the racing feels good, it's easy to get into a match, and it's fun over repeated short sessions.

(Options 1, Exploration/Adventure, and 2, Platformer, were not chosen.)
